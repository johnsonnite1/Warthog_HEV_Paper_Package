Novelty scope
The search did not establish the absence of all earlier H-infinity/MFAC
hybrids. Related hybrid model/data controllers exist, including Xu et al.
2025. Accordingly the manuscript claims an HEV implementation, explicitly
specified actuator-referenced blending arrangement and measured tradeoffs;
it does not claim the first hybrid robust/adaptive controller.
